## Title and Biological Question ##

**Project Title:** Analysis of the WNT5A–ROR2 Signaling Axis in Osteoblast Differentiation

**Biological Question:** How does WNT5A released by osteoblast-lineage cells activate signaling in receiving cells and regulate RUNX2 expression, osteoblast differentiation, and bone formation?

## Chosen Sender Cell and Biological Context ##

**Sender Cell:** Osteoblast

**Biological Context:** Osteoblasts are bone-forming cells responsible for producing and mineralizing the bone extracellular matrix. They communicate with neighboring cells through signaling molecules that regulate osteoblast differentiation, activity, and bone remodeling. WNT5A is an extracellular signaling ligand associated with osteogenic differentiation and bone formation.

## Candidate Ligand and Evidence for Sender-Cell Expression ##

**Candidate Ligand:** WNT5A (Wnt Family Member 5A)

**Sender-Cell Evidence:** WNT5A expression has been reported in osteoblast-lineage cells and during osteogenic differentiation. WNT5A functions as an extracellular signaling ligand and can activate non-canonical Wnt signaling pathways. Its expression in the selected sender cell should be verified using gene-expression databases and published experimental studies.

**Biological Significance:** WNT5A signaling contributes to osteogenic differentiation and can influence RUNX2 expression. RUNX2 is a transcription factor essential for osteoblast development and the regulation of bone-related genes.

## Receptor and Receiver Cell with Supporting Evidence ##

**Receptor:** ROR2 (Receptor Tyrosine Kinase-Like Orphan Receptor 2), potentially functioning with a Frizzled (FZD) receptor.

**Receiver Cell:** Osteoblast or osteoblast-lineage cell.

**Supporting Evidence:** Published studies have demonstrated WNT5A-associated ROR2 activation in human osteoblastic cells. WNT5A–ROR2 signaling is associated with non-canonical Wnt signaling and osteogenic differentiation. The exact receptor complex depends on the cellular context.

**Signaling Context:** Regulation of osteoblast differentiation, RUNX2 expression, and bone formation.

## Functional Interpretation

Osteoblasts can participate in local signaling through WNT5A. When WNT5A binds to an appropriate receptor complex involving ROR2 and potentially a Frizzled receptor, it can activate non-canonical Wnt signaling. Depending on the cellular context, downstream signaling may involve RhoA/ROCK and JNK pathways. These pathways can contribute to the regulation of RUNX2 and other osteogenic genes, supporting osteoblast differentiation and bone formation.

## Final Model and Interpretation ##

In this model, the sender osteoblast releases WNT5A into the extracellular space. WNT5A binds to an appropriate receptor complex on a receiving osteoblast or osteoblast-lineage cell, involving ROR2 and potentially a Frizzled receptor.

Receptor activation initiates non-canonical Wnt signaling. Depending on the cellular context, intracellular signaling may involve Dishevelled proteins, RhoA/ROCK, and JNK. These pathways can influence transcriptional regulation, including RUNX2 expression. RUNX2 then regulates genes required for osteoblast differentiation and the production of bone matrix proteins.

The biological outcome is the regulation of osteoblast differentiation and bone formation. The strength and direction of the response depend on the cell type, developmental stage, receptor composition, and experimental conditions.

Together, these resources help connect extracellular signaling with RUNX2 regulation and osteoblast differentiation.
